:::caution[Paused build, and the shape will change]
First Chair is paused while the drivers get measured. This is the model as of 2026-08-07.
Nothing is print-verified.

**The shape you're looking at is wrong and we know it.** This build was forked from the
over-ear Daily Driver; the dimensions were reset to on-ear but the *form* wasn't
redesigned, so what's in the viewer is essentially a shrunken over-ear — a domed cup and
a circumaural stance on a 54 mm rim. The new form is decided but not modelled yet, so
these parts will change shape. The **baffle** is also still the Daily Driver's 77 mm
plate.

Follow along, and fork freely. It's all at
[github.com/makerphones/first-chair](https://github.com/makerphones/first-chair).

## Two print orientations that aren't optional

Most of the orientation notes above are about surface finish and support. Two of them
are structural:

- **The yoke prints flat in its plane, with the layer lines running *along* the arm.**
  The arms carry bending load and layer adhesion is the weak axis. This is the
  difference between a yoke that lasts and one that snaps.
- **The cup prints grille-face-down.** The lattice is self-supporting that way. The pad
  lip ends up at the top, overhanging by 3 mm, so give it a touch of support or expect to
  clean it up.

## What you don't print

**The bow.** No FDM plastic is a good spring — a printed headband works on day one and
is loose by month three. This is the one bought structural part.

**The pads.** First Chair ships no pad and designs none: the cup rim is built to the
Grado pattern so the whole aftermarket fits. That's deliberate, and it's a feature —
swapping flats for bowls is your main tonal lever, and it costs about ten dollars
instead of a reprint.

**The driver.** 40 mm, ~32 Ω. A mockup appears in the 3D assembly above so the
driver ↔ baffle ↔ clamp fit reads.

For sourcing and rough costs, see the
[bill of materials in the repo](https://github.com/makerphones/first-chair/blob/main/BOM.md). Each printed part is a starting point you
can modify — the model is fully parametric, so changing a driver, a pad, or the head size
regenerates the parts to match.
