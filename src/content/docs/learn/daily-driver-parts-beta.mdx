:::caution[Live work in progress]
This is the **beta** view — the design exactly as it stands right now, rebuilt and
republished on **every commit**. Parts may be mid-change, half-finished, or briefly
broken. For the stable, promoted version, see the
**[current build](/learn/daily-driver-parts/)**.
:::

This is the Daily Driver as it's being worked on **right now**. Drag the slider to **pull
the assembly apart and back together**, toggle the **sub-assemblies** (earcup, gimbal,
headband), or **click any part to isolate it**. It's the same model developed in the open
at [github.com/makerphones/daily-driver](https://github.com/makerphones/daily-driver), so
this page changes as the design does — sometimes several times a day.

<PartsViewer />

When a build here is good enough to be the version everyone sees, it gets **promoted** to
the [current build](/learn/daily-driver-parts/), which stays put until the next promotion.

## Parts to print (live)

These are the live part renders and models — they track the beta above, so treat any print
metadata as provisional until promoted.

For sourcing the bought hardware and rough costs, see the build's bill of materials in the
[design spec](/learn/daily-driver-design-spec/). The model is fully parametric, so changing
a driver, a pad, or the head size regenerates the parts to match.
