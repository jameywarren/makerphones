Bench notes are where the Old Faithful build gets documented in public — the prototypes, the
measurements, the dead ends, and the decisions, posted as they happen rather than after the
story has a tidy ending.

The first entries are being written now. Until they land, the manual's
[design methodology chapter](/learn/design-methodology/) describes the process being followed,
and the [About page](/about/) has the short version of what Old Faithful is.
