Bench notes are where the Daily Driver build gets documented in public — the open-back design,
the prototypes, the measurements, the dead ends, and the decisions, posted as they happen rather
than after the story has a tidy ending.

The Daily Driver is being designed with AI as a design partner; the
[Designing Headphones with AI](/learn/designing-headphones-with-ai/) chapter walks through that
workflow, and the [design methodology](/learn/design-methodology/) chapter describes the build
process being followed. The source files live in the open at
[github.com/makerphones/daily-driver](https://github.com/makerphones/daily-driver), and the
[About page](/about/) has the short version of what the Daily Driver is.

The first entries are being written now.
